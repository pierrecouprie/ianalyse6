Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Overview​

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled in iAnalyse for macOS and iPadOS.

iAnalyse does not collect, use, transmit, or sell personal data.

iAnalyse is a local working application. Projects, media, annotations, drawings, representations, preferences, and other content created or imported in the application remain under your control, on your device or in the storage location you choose.

Data Collected​

iAnalyse does not collect any personal data.

The application does not create user accounts, does not use analytics, does not display advertising, does not track your activity across other apps or websites, and does not use an advertising identifier.

Files and Projects​

When you import audio, video, MIDI, image, PDF, or data files into iAnalyse, those files are used only for the application’s features.

I do not have access to your files, Projects, or their content. They are not sent to an iAnalyse server.

If you choose to save or synchronize your Projects with iCloud, a cloud service, a network drive, or another external location, the handling of those files depends on the service you use and its own privacy terms.

Sharing with Third Parties​

iAnalyse does not share personal data with third parties.

The application does not include third-party services for advertising tracking, analytics, or user data collection.

Retention and Deletion​

Projects, imported files, and preferences are retained locally as long as you keep them on your device or in the storage location you choose.

You can delete your Projects and files from iAnalyse, the Finder, the Files app, iCloud Drive, or the storage service you use. You can also remove local preferences and data by uninstalling the application, depending on macOS or iPadOS behavior.

Because iAnalyse does not collect personal data on a server, there is no iAnalyse account to delete and no personal data to request from an iAnalyse online service.

System Permissions​

iAnalyse may request access to certain files or folders only to open, import, save, or export the items you choose to use with the application.

You can change or revoke certain permissions in macOS or iPadOS settings. If a permission is revoked, some features related to the affected files may stop working until you grant access again.

Children​

iAnalyse does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Contact Me​

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact me at info [at] pierrecouprie.fr.
