Use the Timeline

The timeline displays the temporal organization of the Project. It is used to follow playback, navigate through media, place annotations, display markers, and work with segmentations.

Read the Timeline​

The timeline is read from left to right. The current position is indicated by the playhead. Annotations, markers, segmentations, and temporal representations are displayed around this time scale.

You can show or hide the timeline depending on the type of work you are doing.

Move the Playhead​

Tap in the timeline to place the playhead at a precise position. You can also drag it to browse through the media visually.

When playback is active, the playhead advances automatically. Elements synchronized with time update in the Document.

Zoom and Navigate​

Zoom lets you work at different scales:

  • zoom out to see the whole Project;
  • zoom in to adjust an annotation, marker, or segmentation precisely;
  • follow the playhead to keep the current position visible during playback.

Use the full view when you want to quickly return to the overall structure of the Project.

Select and Edit in Time​

The timeline lets you select time-based elements. Once an element is selected, you can adjust its position, duration, or properties from the inspector.

For time-based annotations, always check the start and end after moving or resizing.

Edit Timeline Properties​

The timeline inspector lets you manage the general display: background, playhead, markers, linked media, waveform, sonogram, representations, and segmentations.

These settings affect the readability of the Project but do not replace the properties of each annotation or representation.
