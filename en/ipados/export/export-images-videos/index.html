Export Images and Videos

Image or video export produces a shareable result from a slideshow, a representation, or a Project view.

Prepare the View to Export​

Before exporting, adjust the view as you want it to appear in the final file: ratio, distribution between Document and timeline, zoom, background, and visible elements.

The exported file uses the visual organization of the displayed view.

Export an Image​

Image export can produce an image of the current view or a series of images. Available formats are:

  • JPEG;
  • HEIC;
  • PNG;
  • TIFF.

Also choose the output size: 720p, 1080p, 4K, or 8K depending on the intended use.

Export a Video​

Video export creates an animated file from the Project. Choose the frame rate, output size, and codec.

Available codecs are:

  • H.264, highly compatible;
  • HEVC, more compact but less universal.

Check the preview before starting the final export.

Tips​

Use a reasonable size for tests, then increase the resolution for the final export. 4K and 8K exports can take longer and produce large files.
