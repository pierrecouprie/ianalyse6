Use Cursors

Cursors guide playback in a slideshow, for example to follow a score, an annotated image, or an area of the Document during playback.

Prepare the Support​

Before creating a cursor, import the visual support to follow: image, PDF, or another element displayed in the slide. Also import the audio or video media that will serve as the time reference.

Then place the support in the slide and check that it is correctly sized.

Create a Manual Cursor​

Place the playhead at the first cursor position, then add a cursor. Choose its shape and the number of required steps or positions.

The cursor appears in the slide and in the timeline. Adjust its visual position in the slide and its time positions in the timeline.

Edit Key Positions​

A cursor uses graphical and temporal key positions. Graphical positions indicate where the cursor appears in the image; temporal positions indicate when it reaches those places.

Adjust both sets of positions until the movement correctly follows the media.

Use Easy Cursor​

Easy Cursor speeds up the creation of regular cursors. First define graphical positions on the support, then start playback and indicate time positions while listening.

This method is especially useful for following a score at a moderate tempo.
